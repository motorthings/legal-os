# Personas

The Legal AI OS serves three kinds of person, and each needs a different framing of the same pages. The nav is organized around *what you're doing right now*, not around the app's internal functions.

Three profiles, one shell, one page set. Each profile is a lens, not a separate app.

---

## 1. Attorney — do the work

Mid-level associate or in-house counsel under hours pressure. Lives inside one practice area.

**Question:** get this done faster, and safely, today.

**Day:** a new employment dispute lands → intake triages it in seconds → research the governing statute → cite-check the brief → draft the separation agreement → review the vendor contract.

**Sees first:** practice areas — Employment, Corporate, Litigation, Regulatory. Then the shared tools — Research, Cite Check, Contract Review, Matter Intake, KM.

**Never touches:** Firm Simulation, Governance, Value Reporting.

**Test:** an attorney in a hurry finds their practice area in one glance. If they scroll past Firm Simulation to reach Employment, it fails.

**Pages:**
- Practice: Employment, Due Diligence, Regulatory
- Tools: Contract Review, Legal Research, Cite Check, Matter Intake, KM Intelligence

---

## 2. Firm Leader — run the firm

GC, CKO, or managing partner. Ops and strategy rolled into one home.

**Question:** is this adopted and clean, and where do we invest next.

**Day:** check the intake queue and adoption numbers → review governance → run a simulation → pull value reporting for the client conversation.

**Sees first:** *Run it* (ops) up front — pipeline, adoption, KM. *Steer and prove* (strategy) behind — governance, simulation, value reporting.

**Never touches:** drafting, arguing, the billable work itself.

**Test:** the leader sees direction and proof, not a pipeline of tools. If their home reads as a function list, it fails.

**Pages:**
- Run it: Dashboard, Matter Pipeline, KM & Adoption
- Steer & Prove: Value Reporting, Firm Simulation

The ops/leader distinction survives as a section header, not a separate profile. Same pages, two questions: ops reads the numbers to fix the machine; the leader reads them to defend the investment upward.

---

## 3. Tour Leader — show it off

Charlie presenting to a recruiter, a peer, a prospective client.

**Question:** make them see the whole thing, and be impressed.

**Day:** one click → a guided path with a through-line → each highlight lands with a reason it matters → close on the audit trail.

**Sees first:** the narrative — three pillars, five layers, each function given its beat. Seeded data so nothing looks empty.

**Never touches:** actual work. It's a performance, not a task.

**Test:** the tour has an arc, not a list. If it's the flat menu with a title, it fails.

**Pages:**
- The Tour: Guides & Diagrams
- Everything: the full function list, shown as breadth
